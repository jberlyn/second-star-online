# Fencing

Fencing is the top tier of sword skill. It adds attack power to swords at high values and rises only once Swordsmanship is above 20.

| | |
|---|---|
| Kind | Weapon |
| Starting value | 1 |
| Rises from | Melee kills; the Paladin and Dragoon promotions |
| Cap | [Combat cap](README.md#caps) |
| Medal | None |
| Counts towards | Bio score, high scores, combat rating |

## How it rises

- **Melee kills** with the Bo, Dark Scimitar, Dark Sword, Fire Sword, Gold Sword, Ice Sword, Katana, Laser Sword, Long Sword, Pharaoh Sword, Rune Sword, Scimitar, Short Sword, Star Sword, Sword of Hope or Unholy Lance. The chance is 1 in (25 − Stamina + Fencing + W), and a gain also needs Swordsmanship above 20. See [melee kills](README.md#melee-kills).
- **Promotion to Paladin or Dragoon** raises Fencing to a roll of 1 to 10 if it is lower.

## What it does

Attack power with any weapon whose name contains "Sword": +1 above 60, +1 above 80 and +2 above 90, up to +4.

## Connected systems

- **Combat:** attack power (the "Sword" block in [attpower.json](../../packages/data/content/attpower.json)).
- **Bio titles:** Master Swordsman (above 80 with Slash above 80 and Swordsmanship above 90).

## Balance notes

- Fencing gives nothing below 61. The Broad Sword and Knight's Sword do not train it, though they benefit from it.
- Like Swordsmanship, high Stamina slows or stops its gains.
