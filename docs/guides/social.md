# Social

## What happens in play

A character can join a guild, follow a faction, keep a buddy list and inspect another player's biography. A murder can mark the killer as wanted, which staff can later answer with `/jail` or `/pardon`; Assassin events track scores for eligible kills. The High Score Statue ranks every player on the server.

## How it works

Opening a Guild Center requires standing still directly in front of it; opening it locks movement. Only a member of a guild can view the guild list's selected record, which can be any guild's; creation, invitations, answers, leaving and leadership actions update guild records through server handlers. The server checks membership and leadership for actions and updates online members when the guild changes; an abdicating leader's screen redraws as a member's. A leader cannot leave, only abdicate or disband, and an accepted invitation joins only while the inviter is online and still leads the guild. At login a character still in its guild rejoins it, and one exiled or whose guild disbanded while they were away is told (`guildLogin`). A guild's halls belong to it by id and its Guild Key doors, vault and board answer to its members ([guild-halls.ts](../../packages/server/src/systems/social/guild-halls.ts)). Disbanding demolishes the guild's halls, after a warning and a second Disband, and empties its vault; while a hall stands the vault must be emptied first. The name is then free for anyone, as a new guild. At start the server matches each older hall to its guild and demolishes any hall whose guild is gone (`settleHalls`). Each faction is a content row keyed by name (see the [modding guide](../modding.md#factions-socialjson)); its power gives its members a tier, and its perks by tier affect combat, spells, skill caps and regeneration (`factionPerk`), and class perks and world-counter perks add beside them where they are read (see [progression](progression.md) and [world](world.md)); loyalty drains while online, warning at set counts before it ends the membership (`loyaltyStep`). Asking about faction power in chat has each leader on the map say how their faction stands (`askFactionPower`). A head turned in to a faction leader who would not take the giver stays in the bag and moves no power. Each faction's announcements reach only its online members, so leaving a faction in any way stops them. Login restores faction state, and death and head turn-ins can change consequences or power. The Assassin's game is content (`assassin` in `social.json`): a world without it has no game, its menu and statue do nothing, and a game left running when a publish removes it ends without lines or prizes (`endRemovedGame`). Assassin event state controls whether kills score, a wanted victim included, and maintains the event's rankings, ties included, and an all-time legends table. A credited killer must still be online when the victim's death lock ends; Lightning makes qualifying melee hits lethal and its deaths flag no one. Ending an event awards configured prizes to up to the first four places, stopping at the first blank prize; an offline winner's prize goes into their stored bag. With each periodic save the server works out the score of every player whose role lacks `noHighScores` (the bio score: the 33 skills on its list in `bios.score.counted`, HPmax and SPmax, with no upper limit) and ranks it into the High Score Statue's top ten, keeping each player's best; the table is stored through [high-score persistence](../../packages/server/src/persistence/highscores.ts) and its texts are in [social content](../../packages/data/content/social.json).

Buddy commands change the account's list, which holds at most 199 names (`BUDDY_MAX`); entry and login/logout presence notify online players and update their Friends windows, and each player's `presenceChatLines` setting (`off`, `friends`, or `everyone`, default `off`) controls whether logon/logoff lines also print in chat. Guild chat (`/g`) shares the chat cooldown and honours ignore lists, and `/invite` invites a player to the guild (subject to the target's `guildInvitesFrom` privacy setting). Bio requests build a view from the target's state and profile, only for a target who is online; closing your own Bio saves a changed profile, cut to 500 characters and refused with an alert if it holds a ":" or "/" (`saveProfile`). The Who window lists everyone online in logon order. Death can invoke murder and faction consequences; arena defeat does not mark a killer. A wanted player is attacked by Officers even on maps where fighting is off, and a Holy Shield hits only wanted players. Staff with `/pardon` can pardon a wanted player they outrank, or anyone if their role has `overrule`; pardoning yourself also needs the `pardonSelf` permission. A jail sentence is stored as an end time and runs in real time whether or not the prisoner is online; dying in jail does not reset it (see [world](world.md)). Chat commands route to these handlers; [chat](chat.md) covers ignore and instant messages, and [progression](progression.md) covers medal checks.
