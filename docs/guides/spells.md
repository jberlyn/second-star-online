# Spells

## What happens in play

A character can learn a spell from a book, then cast it when its requirements are met. A projectile travels through the map and can hit a target; other spells change a character, summon a creature or create an effect on the ground.

## Where it lives

- [packages/server/src/systems/spells/buffs.ts](../../packages/server/src/systems/spells/buffs.ts): counts down the buffs and body changes (Smoke Bomb, Morph, Hold, Stone Skin, Firesword, Magic Shield, the monster-spell block and the timed effects Haste, Bless, Holy Weapon and Weaken) and clears the timed effects at a death.
- [packages/server/src/systems/spells/cast.ts](../../packages/server/src/systems/spells/cast.ts): checks casts, plans and places summons, and sends the other cast messages: the Fire Staff's and Firesword's free fireballs, arrows, a Battleship's cannonball shots and the monster a dropped item releases.
- [packages/server/src/systems/spells/collide.ts](../../packages/server/src/systems/spells/collide.ts): applies collisions to players and monsters, the body changes, Argon's Fist, poison's hits, cannonball hits and the skill a damaging spell or arrow trains.
- [packages/server/src/systems/spells/fx.ts](../../packages/server/src/systems/spells/fx.ts): queues, places and advances spell effects, and sets the monster-spell block.
- [packages/server/src/systems/spells/geometry.ts](../../packages/server/src/systems/spells/geometry.ts): calculates the effect, player and monster rectangles collision uses, and the Holy Shield test.
- [packages/server/src/systems/spells/hazards.ts](../../packages/server/src/systems/spells/hazards.ts): runs the server timer: the Server Spirit's casts and the church bell.
- [packages/server/src/systems/spells/learn.ts](../../packages/server/src/systems/spells/learn.ts): reads a spell book or a skill book.
- [packages/server/src/systems/spells/monsterspells.ts](../../packages/server/src/systems/spells/monsterspells.ts): rolls and aims each monster's spells.
- [packages/shared/src/rules/spells/spellfx.ts](../../packages/shared/src/rules/spells/spellfx.ts): defines effect placement, motion and sprite intersection, the frame each run acts on (`FX_TRIGGER_FRAMES`), the trails and the gathering runs (a harvest node's strike and fishing's cast) that draw on the ground.
- [packages/shared/src/rules/spells/spells.ts](../../packages/shared/src/rules/spells/spells.ts): defines spell costs, crystals, power, schools, targeting, effect amounts, books and the timed effects.
- [packages/shared/src/rules/spells/identify.ts](../../packages/shared/src/rules/spells/identify.ts): works out the lines Identify prints about an item.
- [packages/shared/src/content/fx.ts](../../packages/shared/src/content/fx.ts): lists every effect run the content and the engine draw, and how many frames each shows.
- [packages/shared/src/protocol/spells.ts](../../packages/shared/src/protocol/spells.ts): the `cast` intent and the spell messages.
- [packages/server/src/persistence/spells.ts](../../packages/server/src/persistence/spells.ts): loads and saves a character's spellbook.
- [packages/client/src/ui/spellbook.ts](../../packages/client/src/ui/spellbook.ts): the spellbook window and the armed spell.
- [packages/data/content/spells.json](../../packages/data/content/spells.json): the Classic pack's spells, their effect runs and power tables.

Entry points include `packages/server/src/systems/spells/cast.ts` (`castIntent`), `packages/server/src/systems/spells/fx.ts` (`placeQueued`), `packages/server/src/systems/spells/fx.ts` (`advanceFx`), `packages/server/src/systems/spells/collide.ts` (`playerCollision`), `packages/server/src/systems/spells/collide.ts` (`monsterHits`), `packages/server/src/systems/spells/monsterspells.ts` (`monsterSpells`), `packages/server/src/systems/spells/hazards.ts` (`serverTimer`) and `packages/server/src/systems/spells/learn.ts` (`readBook`).

## How it works

Books can teach spells, and a book is used up only when it teaches something. Reading any book first needs its Mysticism; a combat skill book adds its `points` (Classic: 1 plus `random(5)`) to its skill, stopping at its `upTo` (77) or the reader's own cap for the skill, and a skill on the XP model takes the book's `xp` instead; a spell book adds its spell to the spellbook unless it is already there (`runBook`). The saved spellbook keeps every line that names a spell, however many (`savedSpellbook`); a line whose spell a publish removed is dropped before the next save. Casting checks the caster and spell requirements, including costs and any needed materials. A rider cannot cast. The server checks that the selected spell is in the caster's spellbook and that the click is on the playfield, then makes every refusal (harmful spells on protected ground, what the spell's `requires` rows ask of the caster (a skill, the level or a class), Identify with no item on the clicked tile, SP, crystals, Portal near another player or with no open destination, a summon other than Summon Creature where fighting is off, Raise Dead with no grave on the clicked tile or the one above, a summon off the map or onto a blocked tile, and any summon when all four roster slots are live) before it starts the action delay and removes the crystals and spell points. Crystals come from the last bag line holding that colour's crystal, which must hold enough by itself (`crystalLine`). Power starts at 1 and adds the `power.base` rows (Classic: Magery's tiers), the faction, class and world-counter `spellPower` perks and worn spell gear (`basePower`), then the spell's own power table (Classic: its school's tiers; `spellPower`). Shared rules calculate power and effects, while server handlers apply them. Cast messages are queued and placed during a later world tick, when the caster's own cast trains Magery and then the spell's school (`spellImp`; the free fireballs, arrows and cannonballs train nothing there); effects advance and their sprite geometry determines collisions with players or monsters. An effect acts when its run reaches a set frame (`FX_TRIGGER_FRAMES` lists the last such frame of each: a heal's 3, Crush's 18, Meteor's 19), so a run cut shorter than that never does it. Collision handlers apply damage, healing and other effects. A player's spell or arrow that does damage also trains its owner by the `hit` source (`trainHitter` in collide.ts): an arrow the skills of the bow it left, a spell its schools. The amounts each effect deals or heals, the buffs' lengths and bonuses, Morph's roll and the free fireballs' power are rows of the `balance` dataset, edited on the Control Panel's Spells page (its Damage and buffs tab; `spellEffects`, `spellHits`, `buffs` and `castPower` in [balance.json](../modding.md#balance-balancejson)); a spell row's own `amount` replaces its effect's for that spell's hits; the spell-defense rows in the item rules (`armour.spell`) and Magic Shield (minus 5) change the damage a player takes from Arrows, Crush, Meteor, Fireball, Bolt and Wind Wave; Firebolt and Splash ignore them. A harmful spell or arrow on a rider wears the vehicle's hull by a fixed amount instead (`hullHitDamage`, Classic's 2; see [vehicles](vehicles.md)). Combat and progression handle the resulting deaths and rewards. How many frames of a run show, and its trail's, is worked out in one place for the engine and the Control Panel (`fxRuns` in [packages/shared/src/content/fx.ts](../../packages/shared/src/content/fx.ts)): a point run shows frames 1 to its row's `lastFrame`, a projectile cycles 1 to 3, and the engine's own runs have fixed lengths. A run already playing when a publish shortens it ends at its new last frame. Operators upload a run's frames on the Spells page or the Pictures & Sounds page (see [Spell effects](../running-a-server.md#spell-effects)).

Buffs and transformations use timers and server state. SSO finished the seven spells 1.6 listed but gave no effect (issue #17): Replenish heals between Minor and Major Heal; Haste speeds up HP and SP regeneration; Bless adds attack power; Holy Weapon adds to each melee hit, more on an undead monster; Weaken takes attack off a player or monster; Poison Dart flies like a fireball and poisons the first player or monster it touches, hitting every two seconds for its caster (any heal cures it, and a death ends it); and Identify prints what the item on the clicked tile does, worked out from the item rules (`identifyLines` in [packages/shared/src/rules/spells/identify.ts](../../packages/shared/src/rules/spells/identify.ts)). The lengths are the `buffs` rows' time plus a time for each point of power, unless the cast spell's `lasts` says otherwise (`timedEffectFrames`); they count down in `timedEffectsStep` (buffs.ts), and poison in `poisonPlayerStep` and `afflictedMonstersStep` (collide.ts). Weaken and Poison Dart are harmful: never on yourself, on another player only where PvP is on, and a poison stops hurting where its caster's spells could not. A spell can train more than one skill (`alsoTrains`): Poison Dart trains Black Arts and Martial Arts. A summon uses the first free monster roster slot; Summon Creature places a different monster for a caster whose skill is above the spell's `summon.skilled` line (Classic: a Cow instead of a Pig above 39 Sorcery). Monster spells are chosen in the mover pass for an emulated player; a monster's cast blocks further monster spells for everyone on the map for the balance row `timings.npcSpellBlockFrames` (Classic: 30 frames), a harmful one is never cast where fighting is off, and a `Passive` monster casts its harmful spells only once provoked. The monster spells themselves (`monsters.casting.spells`: tag, effect, aim, power and the 7-step ladder by players on the map) are edited on the Control Panel's [Creatures & NPCs](../running-a-server.md#the-creatures--npcs-page) page's Monster spells card, and a monster casts the ones its NPC file names with `SPL|` lines, which the same page's Loot & spells edits. The server timer runs every 7 ticks (350 ms): it casts the Server Spirit's fireballs and bolts into the `hazardMap` setting's map (unset by default, so none) and counts the church bell, which plays on the `bellMap` setting's map (also unset by default, so no bell; 1.6 rang it on `x999y999`) whether or not a hazard map is set. A Firesword recast replaces the power and timer of the one running, so a weaker recast can shorten it. The client draws and plays sounds for received effects, but the server decides outcomes. Spell content is maintained in [the editable data](../../packages/data/content/).

## Differences from 1.6

The seven spells 1.6 left without an effect work in SSO; see [the difference](../differences-from-fso.md#the-spells-16-left-without-an-effect-work). Among the spell fixes, refused casts cost nothing, armour and Magic Shield change spell damage, schools train and empower every spell, buffs refresh rather than stack, and books, summons and monster spells behave as 1.6 meant. A few odd behaviours stay on purpose (the Whirlwind drain, the Firesword line, Raise Dead's drop below the grave, Passive healers, monsters' random fireballs). The server authors spells and checks their collision; Hold is refused on protected maps. See the [spell bug fixes](../differences-from-fso.md#spells), [kept behaviours](../differences-from-fso.md#kept-on-purpose) and [server-side spell changes](../differences-from-fso.md#server-side-spells).
